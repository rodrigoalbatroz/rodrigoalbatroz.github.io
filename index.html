01 · ABOUT ME

Brazilian roots.
European football.

I'm a Brazilian football coach building my career in European football, with senior and youth experience in Latvia and abroad. From Assistant Coach to Head Coach, I've worked across different competitive environments and football cultures.

I focus on player development, tactical work, a clear game model and strong team culture. Being Brazilian and working in Europe has strengthened my adaptability, communication and ability to build relationships with players from different backgrounds.

My long-term ambition is to become a Head Coach and build teams that compete with clarity, courage and purpose.
